What's new

🦇 Rules 0.10.11: Quick Air

  • Small evasive creatures (too small to count as a Tall threat) now add to Threats: +0.10, +0.05 per power (up to 3), and +0.10 for each mana under 3. A 1-mana 1/1 flier gets +0.35; a 3-mana 2/2 flier gets +0.20.
  • Air density (deck check): Threats + 0.25 × (number of small evasive creatures ÷ their average mana value). Lots of cheap fliers come out in bunches: Blood Suckers' 25 at an average of 2.8 mana gets +2.2; a deck with 4 at 4.5 mana gets +0.2.
  • Biggest change: Blood Suckers' Threats 3 → 5. Double Dragons and Mechanized Mayhem go 8 → 9; everyone else moves a fraction.

❄️🦇🐲🐉 Rules 0.10.10: Four new decks + Needs Repair fixes

  • New decks: Ominous Weather ❄️ (Isu snow tap-down, 50), Blood Suckers 🦇 🧀 (Zoraline Bat lifedrain, 53), Double Dragons 🐲 🧀 (Miirym Dragon copies, 64) and Korvold Eats Everything 🐉 🧀 (Korvold sacrifice, 57). That's 13 decks.
  • 227 new cards saved with their full text, then tagged, Plan-Tagged (C/T) and grouped, then reviewed card by card until a pass needed no changes. Each deck has its own synergy groups: snow permanents and tap-downs; Bats and life-gain drains; Dragons and Dragon payoffs; sacrifice outlets, death payoffs and tokens.
  • Snow-Covered basics now count as basic lands.
  • Needs Repair in the Garage now gives a 🛠️ Fix for every card it shows: a swap that fits the deck's colors and plan, or a reason to keep it.
  • With 13 decks, the second-best deck in each stat (which sets "10") changed, so the old decks moved a little: Grim 74.5, Kykar 69, Thing 67.5.

👑 Rules 0.10.9: Commander engines

  • A cheat effect on a commander (or secret commander) is the deck's planned strategy and always on hand, so it now counts toward Consistency instead of Mana. This applies to every deck. Right now it moves Dr. Eggman's free drops and Marina Vendrell's ETB.
  • Cheats now carry odds: 100% when you decide, 50% when an opponent picks (villainous choices, punishers). The value is scaled by the odds.
  • A commander cheat that repeats every turn also boosts Hit-Rate: every card it can put into play (its Plan Tag C cards) gets +0.5 × odds extra weight. Eggman (repeats every turn, 50%) gives +0.25 to each Robot, Construct and Vehicle.
  • Changes: Mechanized Mayhem Mana 10 → 7, Consistency 8 → 9, Threats 7 → 8, total 54 → 56. House on Haunted Hill Mana 12 → 11, Consistency 8 → 9, total 63 → 63.5.

🔗 Rules 0.10.8: Synergy groups

  • Each deck has 3–5 synergy groups, both creature types and non-creature themes (e.g. Grim: craft types, huge creatures, graveyard; Thing: noncreature spells, counters, extra combats, spell payoffs, trigger copiers, extra-draw package; Marina: Rooms, Curses, enchantment payoffs, Spirits; Mech: Robots & Constructs, Vehicles, artifact payoffs; Kotis: auras & equipment, mutate, combat-damage triggers, casting opponents' cards; Joel: Fungus & Saprolings, death triggers, sac outlets; Turtle: tokens, counters, Food, Ninjas & Turtles; Kykar: instants & sorceries, spell payoffs, spells that target your own creature, fliers; Mathas: life payment, life swaps, bounty, pillowfort).
  • Every card in a group gets a tiny bonus: +0.005 per other card in the group, max +0.15 per group, max 2 groups per card. Each group pays into the stat where it lands (types and tokens → Threats, death triggers → Reach, Curses and pillowfort → Politics, Rooms and enchantress → Cards, artifact payoffs → Mana…).
  • Then every deck got two more card-by-card passes over Plan Tags and group fits: fixes include Silver Surfer (finds Galactus), Condemn and the wipes for Mathas's bounties, Enchantress's Presence and Sanctum Weaver as enchantress cards, Felix and Baleful Eidolon for Kotis, Rites of Flourishing in Thing's extra-draw package, and Aqueous Form's scry cut down (it was over-scored).

🧩 Rules 0.10.7: Plan Tag

  • Plan Tag replaces Feeds the Plan and the hand-picked plan lists. Every card is checked from its own text: C = works alongside the commander (Robots, Constructs and Vehicles for Dr. Eggman; Dinosaurs, Merfolk, Pirates, Vampires and changelings for The Grim Captain; noncreature spells for The Thing and Kykar; auras and equipment for Kotis; tokens for Leonardo and Joel; bounty and removal for Mathas; Rooms and enchantments for Marina). T = fits the deck's main plan beyond the commander (big-creature cheat, spell payoffs, life as a resource, enchantress and Curses, artifact payoffs, mutate, aristocrat drains, go-wide pumps, spellslinger pings).
  • Card value: +0.15 (or +0.25 if it does it every turn), +0.05 more with both tags. It goes where the synergy really lands, often a weak stat: The Thing's spells → Threats, Kykar's spells → Mana (Spirits sacrifice for red), Kotis's gear → Threats, Eggman's Robots → Mana (free to put in), Joel's tokens → Cards and the drain pile → Reach, Mathas's bounties → Cards. Otherwise the card's best stat.
  • Hit-Rate now uses the tags: both tags 1, one tag 0.5, plus tutors and selection; 3 × the odds of seeing two in your first 10 cards + 1 × the odds of three.
  • Plan B counts T-tagged cards (they work without the commander): 1.5 × the odds of seeing two in 10 cards.
  • Swing's mana penalty is now −0.07 per mana above 3.

🔄 Rules 0.10.6: Swing Factor

New Swing tag in Threats for cards that can suddenly flip the standing of a game: Small (one player's spot flips) 0.25, Medium (a player's life or board turns around) 0.5, Large (the whole table resets) 0.75; +0.5 if repeatable, +0.25 at instant speed, −0.05 per mana above 3 (−0.07 from 0.10.7). Examples: Axis of Mortality and Soul Conduit (life swaps), Reverse the Sands, Tragic Arrogance, Quietus Spike, White Sun's Twilight, Rise and Shine. Life Swappin' Threats 1 → 3.

📖 Rules 0.10.5: full card-text review

Every card was re-read from its saved official text and missing tags were added. New: Rebound counts its free second cast at ×0.8 (Recurring Insight, Invisible Force Field, It's Clobberin' Time!). Biggest fixes: Sorcerer Class level 3 burns every opponent per spell, Secret Arcade turns every permanent into an enchantment for the enchantress engines, Tendershoot Dryad's city's blessing makes Saprolings 3/3, Tezzeret's Gatebreaker digs 5, Altar of the Wretched crafts a huge creature and comes back from the graveyard, Majestic Metamorphosis with Zada turns the team into 4/4 fliers, Jinnie Fay turns every token (even Food and Treasure) into a 2/2 or 3/1, plus Maskwood Nexus, Mirror Entity and Taurean Mauler now feed the Throne's craft. About 35 cards changed.

🎯 Rules 0.10.4: Hit-Rate, Plan B and token saturation

  • Plan cards: every deck has 16–22 hand-picked cards that make it do its thing (Grim's fatties and cheat engines, The Thing's draw engines, Mathas's swap pieces, Turtle's token engines…). Each one is also marked for how well it works without the commander.
  • Hit-Rate (Consistency): plan cards + tutors (+1 each) + selection cards (+0.5) + plan cards with Second Life (+0.25, you can use them twice). The odds of seeing at least one in your first 10 cards ×3, plus the odds of seeing two ×1.
  • Plan B (Consistency): the odds of seeing a plan card that still works without the commander, ×1.5.
  • Answer on time (Interaction): the odds of holding an answer by turn 4 (10 cards), +1 at 100%.
  • Closer on time (Finish): the odds of seeing a finisher by turn 7 (13 cards), +0.75 at 100%.
  • Fix: the Threat variety check now counts only real threat kinds (Tall, Wide, Board Power, noncreature threats, commander), not the new Body and Feeds the Plan bonuses.
  • Wide saturation (Threats): once a deck has 10 points of token makers, the rest count half. You can only cast so many token makers in a game. Turtle 15 → 13; Joel and Mech didn't move.

⚰️ Rules 0.10.3: Second Life (graveyard abilities)

Cards that do something again from the graveyard now score that second use: flashback, encore, recasting from the graveyard, and "exile this card from your graveyard" abilities that hand keywords or abilities to another creature. The graveyard effect is scored with the normal formulas at ×0.6 (it has to get there, and you pay again; ×0.4 if it also costs a card), plus 0.15 Cards for the extra use. Symbiote Spider-Man 0.29 → 1.18 (Find New Host gives its dig to a 12-power trampler), Scavenged Brawler 0.73 → 1.0, Seize the Day 0.65 → 1.25, Desperate Ravings 0.9 → 1.39.

🩸 Rules 0.10.2: bloodthirst and cheaper tutors (trial)

  • Bloodthirst creatures are scored at the size they usually enter: power + the counters (X = the deck's usual damage on its own turn), ×0.75 because you have to deal damage first. Indoraptor in the Grim Throne goes from 0.23 to 1.68 (counted as a 9-power menace).
  • Tutor cost penalty is now −0.25 per mana above 1 (was −0.5), on trial. Search for Glory 0.19 → 0.71, Micromancer 0 → 0.58, Buried Alive → 1.09.

🔧 Rules 0.10.1: lifting the weakest cards

  • Graveyard tutors: cards that put what you want into your graveyard (Buried Alive) now count like hand tutors, +0.25 for each extra card they find.
  • Feeds the Plan: +0.15 (or +0.25 if it happens every turn) when a card triggers the commander or a core engine, added to whatever that card is best at: cheap noncreature spells for The Thing and Kykar, tokens for Leonardo, tokens and bodies for Joel's drain, artifacts for Mech, enchantments for Marina, auras and equipment for Kotis, life payments for Mathas, and graveyard fuel for the Throne.
  • Body floor: a creature scoring under 0.5 now counts its body: a little Threats for its power and a little Survival for its toughness.
  • Later wipes are worth 60% of their value instead of losing a flat chunk.
  • Growers: creatures that grow every turn score at their grown size, like the other growers already did. Michelangelo went from 0.65 to 1.7: he grows each turn, has trample, and his Food token triggers Leonardo. Monastery Swiftspear, Frolicking Familiar, Crackdown Construct and Tempest Hart got the same fix.
  • Politics: single-target removal adds +0.10 (Direction: you choose who loses something, up to 8 cards), counterspells add +0.15 (Deals, up to 5), and the rating now uses the square root of Politics points. The low decks moved up (Kykar 1→6, Turtle 1→4) and Life Swappin' came down from 15 to 12.
  • Mana divide is fixed at 4.00 instead of following the second-highest deck, so more than one deck with heavy ramp can pass 10. Truly Fantastic, Marina and Turtle are now 10+.
  • 🔧 Needs Repair moved to the Garage and replaces the old "Weak spots & fixes" box. It picks each deck's five weakest links from three things at once: our score, how often EDHREC decks play the card, and my read of the card in that deck.

🔄 Rules 0.10: rebuilt from the ground up

Every old tag and score was thrown out. The pod is now these 9 decks, and every card was re-tagged with the new formulas over several review passes. Each formula starts from a small base value and adds or subtracts for things like mana value, instant speed, repeatable (engine) and conditions. Old totals aren't shown because the two systems don't compare.

🧮 How a rating is made

  • Each card's tags add points to one or more stats. Deck-level checks (below) add or subtract a little.
  • Divide number: in each stat, the second-highest deck is set to 10 and everyone else is scaled to match. A deck can go past 10 (max 15), but points above 10 count half in the group totals.
  • Groups: Engine (Mana, Cards, Consistency), Offense (Threats, Finish, Reach) and Defense (Interaction, Survival, Politics) are each out of 30. Total is out of 90.
  • Commanders score like any other card. There's no commander multiplier any more.

📐 The nine stats

  • ⚡ Mana: Ramp (lasting ×0.75, temporary ×0.5, +0.25 for putting a land out), Mana Boost, Cheat (skipping costs), Lands (only nonbasics with extra value score). Deck check: land count, how many lands enter untapped, color fixing, and a land-drop simulation.
  • 🃏 Cards: 0.25 base per card drawn, +0.75 for engines, −0.50 for "this turn only".
  • 🎯 Consistency: Tutors and card selection (scry, dig, loot), plus Hit-Rate (the odds of seeing your plan cards in your first 10 cards) and Plan B (the odds of seeing plan cards that still work without your commander).
  • 🗡️ Threats: Tall (big creatures, scaled by power and effective MV), Wide (tokens and engines), Board Power (anthems; capped at 20 boosted power so a single overrun can't explode). Deck check: threat variety.
  • 🏁 Finish (was Clock): cards that end games: bursts, extra combats, extra turns, alternate wins.
  • 🥷 Reach: evasion (flying, trample, unblockable) on your creatures, cards that give evasion, and drain/burn that skips combat.
  • ✨ Interaction: removal, counters, wipes and denial, all ×0.45. Modal cards score every mode. The first wipe in a deck scores full; later wipes count less. Deck check: does it answer creatures, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers, graveyards and spells?
  • 🔰 Survival: blockers, damage prevention, recursion, rebuilding and protection.
  • 🗣️ Politics: three levers, Deals (group hug, monarch, votes), Deterrents (taxes, pillowfort, punishers) and Direction (goad, redirecting). Decks that pull all three levers get a bonus; very salty decks get nudged down. Single-target removal and counterspells add a little, and the rating uses the square root of the points (0.10.1).

🤔 Calls I made while you were away

  • Divide = the second-highest deck in each stat, so one outlier deck can't squash everyone else.
  • Ratings are capped at 15, and Board Power is capped at 20 boosted power.
  • The commanders' text gets no multiplier; they score like any card.
  • New deck icons: 💰 Life Swappin', 🥚 Mechanized Mayhem, 🪶 Kykar, Zada & Feather.
  • X spells use a sensible X for each deck's mana (listed in each takeaway).
