What's new

🔧 Rules 0.10.1: lifting the weakest cards

  • Graveyard tutors: cards that put what you want into your graveyard (Buried Alive) now count like hand tutors, +0.25 for each extra card they find.
  • Feeds the Plan: +0.15 (or +0.25 if it happens every turn) when a card triggers the commander or a core engine, added to whatever that card is best at: cheap noncreature spells for The Thing and Kykar, tokens for Leonardo, tokens and bodies for Joel's drain, artifacts for Mech, enchantments for Marina, auras and equipment for Kotis, life payments for Mathas, and graveyard fuel for the Throne.
  • Body floor: a creature scoring under 0.5 now counts its body: a little Threats for its power and a little Survival for its toughness.
  • Later wipes are worth 60% of their value instead of losing a flat chunk.
  • Growers: creatures that grow every turn score at their grown size, like the other growers already did. Michelangelo went from 0.65 to 1.7: he grows each turn, has trample, and his Food token triggers Leonardo. Monastery Swiftspear, Frolicking Familiar, Crackdown Construct and Tempest Hart got the same fix.
  • Politics: single-target removal adds +0.10 (Direction: you choose who loses something, up to 8 cards), counterspells add +0.15 (Deals, up to 5), and the rating now uses the square root of Politics points. The low decks moved up (Kykar 1→6, Turtle 1→4) and Life Swappin' came down from 15 to 12.
  • Mana divide is fixed at 4.00 instead of following the second-highest deck, so more than one deck with heavy ramp can pass 10. Truly Fantastic, Marina and Turtle are now 10+.
  • 🔧 Needs Repair moved to the Garage. It picks each deck's five weakest links from three things at once: our score, how often EDHREC decks play the card, and my read of the card in that deck.

🔄 Rules 0.10: rebuilt from the ground up

Every old tag and score was thrown out. The pod is now these 9 decks, and every card was re-tagged with the new formulas over several review passes. Each formula starts from a small base value and adds or subtracts for things like mana value, instant speed, repeatable (engine) and conditions. Old totals aren't shown because the two systems don't compare.

🧮 How a rating is made

  • Each card's tags add points to one or more stats. Deck-level checks (below) add or subtract a little.
  • Divide number: in each stat, the second-highest deck is set to 10 and everyone else is scaled to match. A deck can go past 10 (max 15), but points above 10 count half in the group totals.
  • Groups: Engine (Mana, Cards, Consistency), Offense (Threats, Finish, Reach) and Defense (Interaction, Survival, Politics) are each out of 30. Total is out of 90.
  • Commanders score like any other card. There's no commander multiplier any more.

📐 The nine stats

  • ⚡ Mana: Ramp (lasting ×0.75, temporary ×0.5, +0.25 for putting a land out), Mana Boost, Cheat (skipping costs), Lands (only nonbasics with extra value score). Deck check: land count, how many lands enter untapped, color fixing, and a land-drop simulation.
  • 🃏 Cards: 0.25 base per card drawn, +0.75 for engines, −0.50 for "this turn only".
  • 🎯 Consistency: Tutors and card selection (scry, dig, loot). Hit-Rate is still pending, so this stat is noisy for now: decks with no tutors land at 1. Graveyard tutors count like hand tutors (0.10.1).
  • 🗡️ Threats: Tall (big creatures, scaled by power and effective MV), Wide (tokens and engines), Board Power (anthems; capped at 20 boosted power so a single overrun can't explode). Deck check: threat variety.
  • 🏁 Finish (was Clock): cards that end games: bursts, extra combats, extra turns, alternate wins.
  • 🥷 Reach: evasion (flying, trample, unblockable) on your creatures, cards that give evasion, and drain/burn that skips combat.
  • ✨ Interaction: removal, counters, wipes and denial, all ×0.45. Modal cards score every mode. The first wipe in a deck scores full; later wipes count less. Deck check: does it answer creatures, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers, graveyards and spells?
  • 🔰 Survival: blockers, damage prevention, recursion, rebuilding and protection.
  • 🗣️ Politics: three levers, Deals (group hug, monarch, votes), Deterrents (taxes, pillowfort, punishers) and Direction (goad, redirecting). Decks that pull all three levers get a bonus; very salty decks get nudged down. Single-target removal and counterspells add a little, and the rating uses the square root of the points (0.10.1).

🤔 Calls I made while you were away

  • Divide = the second-highest deck in each stat, so one outlier deck can't squash everyone else.
  • Ratings are capped at 15, and Board Power is capped at 20 boosted power.
  • The commanders' text gets no multiplier; they score like any card.
  • New deck icons: 💰 Life Swappin', 🥚 Mechanized Mayhem, 🪶 Kykar, Zada & Feather.
  • X spells use a sensible X for each deck's mana (listed in each takeaway).
