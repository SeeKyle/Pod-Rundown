What's new

🧪 Rules 0.9.5: goldfish mana, real X values, attack survival

  • Turn-7 mana: every deck was goldfished 100 times (London mulligan, land each turn, ramp cast cheapest first). The average mana on turn 7 ranges from 5.5 (Our Greatest Ally) to 8.1 (Truly Fantastic), and it's shown on every takeaway.
  • X costs: every X, XX and XXX (spells and abilities) now uses that deck's turn-7 mana. Elvenking's Court's Doppelgang only reaches X = 1, Ally's Martial Coup and TMNT's White Sun's Twilight can't wipe on average, and Mind into Matter draws 6.
  • Attack triggers: cards that trigger when they attack are rated by how likely they survive combat. Safe (×1.15) means big, evasive or indestructible, Even is average, and Risky (×0.85) means small ground creatures that trade.
  • New takeaways: every deck's takeaway was rewritten from scratch: how a game goes, ways to win, what beats it, and piloting tips. The Garage has new Heavy Hitters and The Dream sections.

🏹 Rules 0.8: old-school combat gets credit

The rules are now numbered 0.8, since the system isn't fully released yet. General Oldschool goes from 50 → 59, and no other deck's total changed.

  • Banding has an offense tag (your band can't be picked apart by blockers) and a defense tag (you split the attacker's damage, so your blockers live).
  • Provoke counts as repeatable pseudo-removal: it forces a chosen creature to block.
  • Combat Tappers are a new family: repeatable abilities like Heavy Ballista, Maze of Ith or Trap Runner that kill creatures or rewrite a combat. Oldschool runs 19 of them, and General Jarkeld's game plan is now built around them.
  • Shadow counts as unblockable.
  • Animators (rules 0.9) are cards that turn your other stuff into creatures. Basic means one at a time, or until end of turn only (Llanowar Loamspeaker, Peacewalker Colossus, Cyberdrive Awakener). Proficient means whole groups or a new creature every turn (Bello, Iron Man (Futurist Paragon), Donatello (Mutant Mechanic)). Bello used to count as a token maker. No totals changed: Skynet traded a point of Clock for two of Threats.
  • Rules 0.9.2: big scary threats (must-answer creatures and noncreature threats) and animators are worth about 25% more. Plain token makers are worth a little less. Decks built on big threats moved up; the Last of Us and the Haunted House dropped a bit.
  • Rules 0.9.3: big one-time burn and drain now scales with damage: 6 total damage is worth 0.75, plus 0.15 for each point above that. Blood for the Blood God! and Tellah (24 damage each) jumped. X spells use X = 10 in decks with great ramp, otherwise 8. Multi-X spells are fixed at XX = 5 and XXX = 2.
  • Protection has three tiers: one color, several colors, or creatures and everything. The wider tiers also count as evasion.

🐉 New deck: Cheez' Feast for a King

Cheez' Korvold aristocrats deck joins the pod at 70.5 (#11 of 15). It has the best Mana in the pod (19): 20 Treasure makers, with Academy Manufactor, Jolene and Xorn multiplying every one. It eats all of that through 16 sac outlets, and every sacrifice grows Korvold and draws a card. Heads-up: the tag rules score Treasure as ramp, so some of Korvold's card draw shows up under Mana instead of Cards. Big threats are thin outside Korvold, and only two cards answer artifacts or enchantments.

  • 🐉 Jolene sacrificing five Treasures = 5 Korvold draws, 5 Mayhem Devil pings, and 5 drains each from Marionette Apprentice and Mirkwood Bats.
  • 🐉 Ziatora flinging a grown Korvold is the finisher. Reassembling Skeleton + Ashnod's Altar + Pitiless Plunderer loops forever, but infinite combos aren't scored.

Every deck is now scored from card tags instead of hand-picked numbers. Each card gets short tags for what it does (ramp, removal, theft, token copies and so on); every tag has a fixed point value, and the deck's stats are built from those. When a rule changes, the tags get re-run instead of re-judging every card.

🆕 New things the scores now see

  • Theft — stealing once, keeping it for good, or stealing every turn all score differently, and stealing several cards at once scales up. Stolen creatures with haste add Clock.
  • Opponent Gas — casting your opponents' cards (Kotis, Villainous Wealth, Gonti) now counts as card advantage.
  • Doublers — Doubling Season, Teysa, Fractured Realm and friends get a flat value, and every card they boost gets a small "Doubled" bonus (+5% each). Lands never get it.
  • Token copies — cards that make token copies of permanents (Ultron, Rite of Replication, Ivy copying auras) have their own tags and aren't treated as doublers.
  • Commander Game Plans — commanders no longer count double. Each one gets a multiplier from what its own text does and how much the deck uses it (Core +50%, Supported +25%, Side +0%). Partners split it 70/30.
  • Deck bonuses — more ways to win (combat, drain, alt win, combos, mill, commander damage) adds Clock; every deck gets a Consistency bonus that grows with redundancy — 1.5 points, +6% for each of 18 roles (removal, tokens, tutors, pumps…) it has 5+ cards in, +8% for 10+; decks short on mana fixing get a Mana penalty.
  • X spells are scored as if you put 10 mana into X. Big spells (6+ mana) take a small penalty.
  • Symmetric cards (wheels, group hug — things that help everyone) now take only a 10% cut instead of losing half their value (rules 1.1).
  • Presence is now Politics — aimable effects, pillowfort, goad, deterrents and deal-making.

🔍 Coolest finds from the card-by-card reviews

  • 🧪 Heartless Hidetsugu + Wound Reflection takes every opponent to 0 or 1 life.
  • 🧝 Lathril + Seedborn Muse (or Murkfiend Liege) with 10 elves drains each opponent for 10 on every player's turn.
  • 🏴‍☠️ Ardent Plea and Shardless Agent always cascade into the Throne — it's the only nonland card under 3 mana.
  • 👻 Freed from the Real on Marina lets her lock and re-unlock a door over and over: repeat Cramped Vents, Drowned Diner or a 6/6 Demon.
  • 🐍 Quietus Spike + double strike + Felix can make a player lose half their life four times in one combat.
  • 🦋 Doubling Season + Liliana, Death's Majesty — she enters at 10 loyalty and wipes every non-Zombie right away.
  • 🧱 Felothar sacrificing Tree of Redemption draws 13 and discards nothing; Canopy Gargantuan doubles every wall each upkeep.
  • 🍄 South Wind Avatar roughly doubles the whole drain plan: every bit of lifegain makes each opponent lose 1 more.
  • 🤖 Curie copying Panther Robot, or made a base 5/5 by Iron Man, draws 5–8 cards a hit.
  • 🪨 Rites of Flourishing + The Unagi draws you 2 on every opponent's turn.

🛠️ Fixes

  • Orzhov Advokist never actually triggered Kros (players put those counters on their own creatures).
  • Angrath's Marauders doubles damage, not life loss, so it doesn't double Blood Tyrant's drain.
  • Lots of small tag fixes: wrong "Asymmetric" wipes, Flying on a non-creature, a Big Spell penalty on a Room whose bigger door costs 4, and more.

📈 Why the totals went up

Scores climbed for almost every deck because the tags now count synergy, combos, doublers and deck-level bonuses that the old system ignored. The divide numbers that turn points into ratings were kept exactly the same on purpose, so every deck is measured on the same ruler as before. Each deck tab shows its old total next to the new one.
