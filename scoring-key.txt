==================================================
SECTION 1 - HOW THE SCALE WORKS
==================================================
- 9 stats in 3 groups. Every stat aims for 10 (10 = a strong deck).
- Target meaning: 6 = community-average deck, 10 = strong deck.
- Groups: ENGINE, OFFENSE, DEFENSE. Each group is out of 30. Total is out of 90.
- A stat can go above 10. It shows as "10+" on the graph.
  In group totals, points above 10 count HALF (a 12 counts as 11).
- Every card gets points from each stat's formula.
- Stat rating = total points / that stat's "points per 1",
  rounded to the nearest whole number (.5 rounds up), minimum 1.
- Commanders count x2 in every stat. No other commander bonuses
  unless a stat says so (Mana, Cards).
- Infinite combos never count. Cards only combine with cards in the same deck.
- "Expected damage" = per opponent, per turn cycle.

NUDGES (checklist scores, added as POINTS before converting)
  checklist 8+ ........ +1 point
  checklist 5 or less . -1 point
  Curve Fit -> Mana | Threat Variety -> Threats | Plan B -> Survival

SYNERGY (per card - payoffs scale with their fuel)
  A PAYOFF = lord, "whenever a [type/theme] enters/attacks/dies/is cast",
  or type-matters draw / cost reduction / tutoring.
  An ENABLER = a card of that type or theme in the same deck.
  Each payoff keeps its normal points, PLUS 0.05 per enabler in the deck,
  up to +0.5. Commander payoffs get the bonus x2.
  Enablers get NO extra points.
  The bonus goes to the stat the payoff's effect feeds:
    anthem / attack payoff -> CLOCK      tribal draw -> CARDS
    ramp / cost reduction  -> MANA       tutoring    -> CONSISTENCY
    damage / evasion       -> REACH      growth      -> THREATS
    removal / tapping      -> INTERACTION  tokens/protection -> SURVIVAL

EARLY GAME (flat deck bonus, small on purpose)
  Board start: 12+ board-affecting permanents (creatures, token makers,
    engines - NOT mana rocks or lands) at mana value 3 or less
    (cheap modes count) ................ +0.5 point to CLOCK
  Flexible early plays: 6+ instants / flash / modal / cheap-mode plays
    at mana value 3 or less ............. +0.25 point to CONSISTENCY
  (Both thresholds = the deck makes these plays by turn 4 in most games.)

COLORS
  Red 1-4 | Yellow 5-8 | Green 9-10 | Blue 10+

SALT (not part of the total)
  Sum of each card's EDHREC salt score. Lands that only make mana = 0.

==================================================
SECTION 2 - ANTI-OVERLAP RULES
==================================================
A card may score in several stats, but each FEATURE of a card
scores in ONE stat only:
  Haste / flash ................................ CLOCK only
  Evasion (flying, trample, menace, unblockable) REACH only
  Hexproof / ward / indestructible on ITSELF ... THREATS only
  Protecting OTHER cards or your board ......... SURVIVAL only
  Amount of damage per turn .................... CLOCK only
    (REACH only gives a flat "unavoidable" credit)
  Extra cards (draw, play from top/graveyard
    that nets cards) ........................... CARDS
  Finding / choosing / digging for a card ...... CONSISTENCY
  Spell taxes, stax, graveyard hate ............ INTERACTION
  Attack taxes, pillow fort, deterrents ........ TABLE PRESENCE
  Lifegain ..................................... SURVIVAL (not a deterrent)
MODAL "choose one" cards: full value in their best stat,
  HALF value in any other stat.
Exception (user choice): tutors that can find a survival card also give
  a small Survival credit.

==================================================
SECTION 3 - THE 9 STATS
==================================================
ENGINE:   MANA (how fast can I cast my stuff?)
          CARDS (how long can I keep doing stuff?)
          CONSISTENCY (how often do I find the right stuff?)
OFFENSE:  THREATS (how scary is my board?)
          CLOCK (how fast do I kill?)
          REACH (how much of my damage gets through?)
DEFENSE:  INTERACTION (how well do I stop them?)
          TABLE PRESENCE (how well do I make them pick someone else?)
          SURVIVAL (how well do I survive and recover?)

--------------------------------------------------
STAT 1 - MANA (Engine)
--------------------------------------------------
What counts: ramp, rocks, dorks, land ramp, rituals, cost cheats.
Per card:
  base ............................................ 0.5
  + puts a lot of value into play for its cost ..... 0.25
    (makes at least as much mana as it costs, or 4+ mana/lands)
  + any extra bonus (fixing, draw, land to hand...) 0.25
  one-shot ramp (rituals, cascades, one-time cheats) = half value
Commander: x2. Commander that ramps on its own: +0.25 to the total.
Fixing lands (the only lands that score):
  untapped land making 2+ of your colors (shock, check, pain, fast,
    filter, battle, slow lands) ........................ 0.15
  any-color land (Command Tower, Exotic Orchard,
    City of Brass, Spire of Industry, triomes) ......... 0.15
  fetch that finds 2+ of your colors ................... 0.1
  tapped dual / tapped tri / bounce land ............... 0.05
  basics and colorless utility lands ................... 0
  x color need: mono x0 | 2 colors x0.5 | 3 colors x0.75 | 4-5 colors x1
Land-drop bonus (simulated from lands + land-fetching ramp + draw engines):
  misses at most ONE land drop through turn 6
    (5+ lands by turn 6) in 70%+ of games ................. +1 to total
  ...and also has 7+ lands by turn 8 in 70%+ of games ..... +1.25 instead
Nudge: Curve Fit (+1 / -1 point).
  Curve Fit checklist (start 5): +1 avg MV 3.5 or less, +1 enough lands
  (36+, or 33+ with 12+ ramp), +1 lots of cheap modes;
  -1 avg MV 4.5+ with no cheap modes.
  (Fixing is scored by the fixing lands above, not here.)
Points per 1: 1.75

--------------------------------------------------
STAT 2 - CARDS (Engine)
--------------------------------------------------
What counts: draw, value engines, wheels, and play-from-top/graveyard
effects that net extra cards.
Per card:
  base ............................................ 0.25
  + repeatable engine .............................. 0.75
  + big burst (3+ cards or a whole new hand) ........ 0.75
  + any extra bonus (removal, body, ramp...) ........ 0.25
  cantrips and loot/rummage = half value
Commander: x2. Repeatable-draw commander: +0.5 strong / +0.25 moderate.
Points per 1: 2.05  (harshened 2026-10-22: max 3 decks over 10)

--------------------------------------------------
STAT 3 - CONSISTENCY (Engine)
--------------------------------------------------
What counts: how the deck FINDS what it needs (tutors, selection,
digging, choosing from the top or graveyard).
Per card:
  base ............................................ 0.25
  + real tutor (gets a specific card) ............... 1.0
  + big deck manipulation ........................... 0.75
    (choose from top, cast from graveyard with self-mill, dig 4+)
  + repeatable ...................................... 0.5
  + any extra bonus ................................. 0.25
  On-theme / tribal cards = 0 per card, BUT see archetype depth below.
Commander: x2.
Archetype depth (deck-level, added 2026-10-23): count the nonland cards
  that fit the deck's archetype (its main card type or job: tokens, auras/
  equipment, enchantments, elves, artifacts, noncreature spells...).
  +0.5 point for every full 5 such cards (36 cards = +3.5).
Points per 1: 1.0

--------------------------------------------------
STAT 4 - THREATS (Offense)
--------------------------------------------------
What counts: big bodies (5+ power, or 4+ with trample/flying/double strike)
or big repeatable abilities that need an answer.
Per card:
  base ............................................ 0.5
  + must-answer (takes over or ends the game alone) . 0.75
  + hard to answer (hexproof, indestructible, ward,
    can't be countered, comes back) ................. 0.5
  + big enter effect or snowballs ................... 0.25 ONCE
    (haste/flash NOT counted here - they live in Clock)
Commander: x2.
Nudge: Threat Variety (+1 / -1 point).
  Threat Variety = number of threat card types: 1=3, 2=5, 3=7, 4=8, 5=9, 6=10.
Points per 1: 1.48  (harshened 2026-10-22: max 3 decks over 10)

--------------------------------------------------
STAT 5 - CLOCK (Offense)
--------------------------------------------------
What counts: how FAST the deck kills.
Per card:
  base: creature .................................. 0.25
  base: non-creature .............................. 0.15
  + big swing (extra combat, double damage,
    overrun, +X/+X turn, one-turn lethal) ........... 1.0
  + team-wide (hits your whole board) ............... 1.0
  + speed traits (haste, flash, cheap to equip) ..... 0.25
  + expected damage (damage engines) ......... 0.5 per damage per turn
Fuel for sacrifice-based damage (e.g. Ellie):
  fodder makers in deck: under 8 = 0.5 uses/turn, 8-14 = 1, 15+ = 2
Commander: x2.
Diminishing returns: a single card's Clock points above 1.5 (3 for a
  commander) count half.
Points per 1: 2.25  (retuned: pod median 7, then max 3 decks over 10)

--------------------------------------------------
STAT 6 - REACH (Offense)
--------------------------------------------------
What counts: how SURE the damage is to land.
Per card:
  base: evasion or unavoidable/non-combat damage .... 0.25
  base: mills 5+ regularly, or applies poison ....... 0.5
  base: gives OTHER creatures unblockable ........... 0.5
        (also "opponents' creatures can't block")
  + evasive creature with 6 power ................... 1.0
    + per power over 6 .............................. 0.25
    + if it is expected to grow ..................... 0.15
  + repeatable unavoidable damage (flat) ............ 0.5
    + per damage-booster card in the deck ........... 0.15
  + one-shot burn that can hit PLAYERS ...... 0.15 per point of damage
    (damage only to creatures/planeswalkers does not count)
Commander: x2.
Points per 1: 1.75

--------------------------------------------------
STAT 7 - INTERACTION (Defense)
--------------------------------------------------
What counts: everything that removes or stops stuff.
Per card (best mode):
  damage a target ................................. 0.25
    + deals 5+ damage on average .................... 0.5
  destroy a target ................................ 0.5
  exile a target .................................. 0.75
  counterspell .................................... 0.5 (hard counter 0.75)
  steal ........................................... 0.25
    + permanent (not until end of turn) ............. 0.5
  edict (one creature) ............................ 0.25
    + each additional creature sacrificed ........... 0.25
  bounce / tap-down ............................... 0.15
  spell taxes, stax, graveyard/artifact hate ...... 0.25
  + repeatable ..................................... 0.25
  + instant speed ................................... 0.25
  + flexible (hits more than one card type) ......... 0.25
  + extra targets: 2nd target 0.5, each one after 0.25
    (rebound / replicate count as extra targets)
BOARD WIPES (no target bonuses):
  first wipe in the deck .......................... 1.0
  each wipe after the first ....................... 0.5
  + one-sided (you keep your stuff or choose) ....... 0.5
Points per 1: 1.5

--------------------------------------------------
STAT 8 - TABLE PRESENCE (Defense)
--------------------------------------------------
What counts: keeping the hits from coming to you - steering the table
without removing anything. All deterrents live here.
Per card:
  base ............................................ 0.25
  + goad/redirection, one target .................... 0.25
    + each target after the first ................... 0.25
  + hit-all goad (e.g. Taunt from the Rampart) ...... 0.75
  + can't attack you / pillow fort / attack taxes ... 0.5
  + punishers / deterrents .......................... 0.25
  + deal-making (votes, gifts, monarch,
    curses aimed at one player) ..................... 0.25
  + lopsided group hug (helps you more) ............. 0.25
  + repeatable ...................................... 0.25
  purely symmetric effects = half value
  Removal stays in Interaction.
Bargaining leverage (added 2026-10-23): ANY spell or ability that can
  target an opponent, another player, or something they control (harmful
  or helpful: removal, counterspells, taps, steals, curses, pumps,
  "target player draws"...) gets the 0.25 base here, because you can
  bargain with it. Cards that can only target your own stuff don't count.
  (A card already scoring Presence keeps its score; the base isn't doubled.)
Commander: x2.
Points per 1: 1.0

--------------------------------------------------
STAT 9 - SURVIVAL (Defense)
--------------------------------------------------
What counts: surviving and recovering when you DO get hit.
Per card:
  base ............................................ 0.25
  + protects one OTHER thing ........................ 0.25
  + protects several things / whole board .......... 0.5
    (a card protecting only itself scores in Threats, not here)
  + fog / damage prevention ......................... 0.25
  + recursion, one card ............................. 0.5
    + each extra card ............................... 0.25
  + mass recursion .................................. 1.5
    (somewhat symmetrical ........................... 0.75 instead)
  + rebuilding ............ 0.15 per token/body made each turn
  + lifegain ........ 0.15 per 3 life expected per turn cycle
    (one-shot lifegain = half)
  + repeatable ...................................... 0.25
  + instant speed ................................... 0.25
  + gives survival keywords to OTHER creatures ...... 0.25
    (lifelink, indestructible, hexproof, ward, protection...)
    + if it lasts (not a one-off buff) .............. 0.25
  Tutors that can find a survival card in the same deck ... 0.15 each
Commander: x2.
Nudge: Plan B (+1 / -1 point).
  Plan B = how the deck works if its commander or key piece is removed
  (9-10 commander is a bonus, 7-8 works without it, 5-6 weakened,
   3-4 key plan breaks, 1-2 deck folds).
Diminishing returns: a single card's Survival points above 1.25 (2.5 for a
  commander) count half.
Points per 1: 1.85  (retuned: pod median 7, then max 3 decks over 10)

SECTION 3B - HOW TO JUDGE THE GRAY AREAS
==================================================
TYPICAL ATTACKING POWER
  = printed power + buffs that reliably apply the turn it attacks
    (its own triggers, the commander's anthem, usual counters).
  Do NOT assume extra equipment/auras/one-off pump spells.
  Example: The Thing 5/5 + four counters from a noncreature spell = 9.

EXPECTED DAMAGE PER TURN
  = average damage to ONE opponent over one full turn cycle.
  Use real deck averages where possible
    (e.g. Vial Smasher = average mana value of the deck's spells).
  Triggers that need a condition: count how often the deck meets it
    (about 1 noncreature spell per turn unless the deck clearly does more).
  Fuel-based effects use the fuel tiers (under 8 / 8-14 / 15+ makers).

MUST-ANSWER (Threats)
  = if nobody deals with it, it wins the game or locks the table
    within about 2 turns by itself. A big creature that just hits hard
    is NOT must-answer unless it also takes over (steals, wipes, extra
    turns, huge repeatable value, alt win).

HARD TO ANSWER
  = hexproof, shroud, indestructible, ward, protection from most removal,
    can't be countered, or returns from the graveyard on its own.

BIG BODY
  = 5+ typical power, or 4+ with trample / flying / double strike.

BOARD-AFFECTING PERMANENT (early game)
  = creature, token maker, or engine that does something on its own.
    Mana rocks, lands and do-nothing enchantments don't count.

REPEATABLE
  = works every turn or every time a common trigger happens
    (static abilities, upkeep/attack/cast triggers, reusable activations).
  Rebound, flashback, buyback = counts as a second use, not repeatable.

ONE-SHOT vs PERMANENT
  Spells are one-shot unless they leave something on the battlefield.
  Sagas count each chapter separately.

MODAL CARDS
  Pick the mode that scores best; that stat gets full value,
  every other stat it touches gets half.

WHEN UNSURE
  Pick the lower value and note it, so scores don't inflate.
