POD RUNDOWN - CARD TAG SYSTEM  (0.9.5 - Oct 24, 2026 - pre-release)
Works with Pod_Rundown_Scoring_Key.txt (stat meanings, deck-level rules,
divide numbers). For card points, THIS file wins.
=====================================================================
What a card DOES, in short tags. Each card gets its tags once; the
scores get built from the tags. When a rule changes, we re-run the
tags, not the card text.

HOW TO READ THIS
  [B]  Base tag  - says what the card does. Gives the main points.
  [+]  Bonus tag - how WELL it does it. Adds on top of its base tag.
  [x]  Modifier  - multiplies or adjusts the card's other tags.
  [-]  Flag      - no points by itself, but other rules use it.
  PICK ONE = only the highest tag in that group goes on a card.
  Stat codes: MA Mana | CA Cards | CO Consistency | TH Threats |
              CL Clock | RE Reach | IN Interaction | TP Politics |
              SU Survival
  Numbers are points before the divide numbers (divides stay FIXED).

GENERAL RULES
  - Tags stack unless a group says PICK ONE.
  - Tags go on by strict card text AND realistic potential in its deck.
    Synergy with the rest of the decklist can add or remove tags.
  - "Engine" = a permanent that sticks and keeps doing it.
    "One-Off" = happens once (spell, ETB, single trigger).
  - Engine averages use a TWO-TURN window.
  - Commander: scores like any card, then gets a GAME PLAN multiplier
    (see Section 15): Core +50%, Supported +25%, Side +0%, traits +25%.
  - SCORING ORDER (one card, then the deck):
      1. Add up the card's tag points per stat (PICK ONE groups apply).
      2. Card modifiers: Theft amount/Until End of Turn, Conditional, Symmetric,
         Speed, Self-Damage, Big Spell.
      3. Per-card diminishing returns (Clock over 1.5, Survival over
         1.25 count half).
      4. Commander multiplier (commanders only).
      4b. Doubled N (cards boosted by a doubler in the deck): x(1+0.05N).
      5. Deck limits (best 14 ramp / best 12 draw full, rest half).
      6. Deck-level bonuses: wipes (first full, later half), archetype
         depth, early game, curve/variety nudges, land drops (scoring
         key), then Win Routes, Color Strain, Redundancy (Section 16).
      7. Divide numbers, rounding, groups (scoring key).
  - X COSTS: from the deck's goldfish turn-7 mana (Section 18).
  - Planeswalkers, Sagas, Rooms, Adventures: tag each ability, chapter,
    door or half as its own effect. A planeswalker ultimate gets
    Conditional (you rarely reach it).
  - Voltron gear: equipment that can move every turn = Engine buffer;
    auras (fall off when the creature dies) = One-Off buffer.
  - Mana dorks get no extra wipe penalty; the 14-card ramp limit
    already covers decks stuffed with ramp.
  - Per-card diminishing returns stay: Clock above 1.5 and Survival
    above 1.25 count half.
  - RARITY GUIDE (picks the starting tier, never points by itself):
      Common -> Basic | Uncommon -> Basic, can rise to Proficient |
      Rare -> Proficient | Mythic -> Proficient, can rise to Extreme.
      Card text can move it up or down (Sol Ring stays Extreme).
  - A card scores fully in every stat it fits. No spread penalty:
    multi-role cards already help more stats, which is their bonus.
  - DECK LIMITS: best 14 ramp cards full, the rest count half.
                 best 12 draw cards full, the rest count half.
                 (weakest cards take the half hit first)

1. MODIFIERS  (apply after all other tags on the card)
=====================================================================
  [x] Conditional ............ card's other tags x0.75
        needs setup: "as long as", vehicles needing crew, threshold,
        "if you control X". The DECK can lift it (a vehicle in a
        deck full of crew loses this tag).
  [x] Hard Conditional ....... card's other tags x0.5
        setup is tough or rare in that deck.
  [x] Symmetric .............. the card's points x0.9 (changed 1.1:
        was x0.5 - a 10% cut for also helping opponents)
  [x] Needs Fodder ........... becomes Conditional / Hard Conditional
        if the deck can't feed it well (judge from the list).
  [x] Self-Damage/Life Cost .. SU -0.1
  [x] Big Spell 6+ ........... -0.15 at 6 mana, -0.15 more per mana
        over 6; each of the card's Base/Bonus tags gives back +0.05
        (penalty never goes positive). Taken from the card's best
        stat. Printed cost; most expensive half of rooms/splits.

15. COMMANDER GAME PLAN TAGS (commanders only, 2026-10-24)
=====================================================================
  The commander first scores normally with its regular tags (x1, like
  any card). Then it gets Game Plan tags for things its OWN text does
  every game. Each tag is marked with how much THE DECK uses it:

    [Core]       the deck is built around it (about 15+ cards feed or
                 pay it off) ............................... +50%
    [Supported]  the deck has some help for it (about 5-14 cards)  +25%
    [Side]       the commander can do it, the deck doesn't care
                 (under 5 cards) ........................... +0%
    Traits (Hard to Kill, Cheap Recast, Instant Impact) are always
    +25% - they help no matter what the deck does.

  Commander multiplier = 1 + all its boosts added up. NO CAP.
  It multiplies the commander's own points after per-card diminishing
  returns (step 4 of the scoring order). Game Plan tags never count
  toward Redundancy. The deck file shows: Commander Multiplier xN
  Levels are a judgment call; Section 16 family counts are a guide.
  Re-check levels whenever the deck changes.
  PARTNERS (2026-10-24): each partner gets its own tags. The MAIN partner
  (bigger boost / the one the deck is built around) keeps 70% of its
  boost; the SECOND partner keeps 30%. Base points stay x1 for both.
  Example: boost +1.5 -> main x2.05 (1 + 1.5 x 0.7).

  Write tags like:  Game Plan: Voltron [Core]

  Engine:   Ramp, Treasure, Cost Cheating, Lands Matter, Draw, Card
            Selection, Tutor, Cast From Graveyard, Cast Opponents' Spells
  Offense:  Tokens, Counters, Team Pump, Voltron, Evasion, Burn/Drain,
            Extra Combat, Card Eater, Poison, Alt Win
  Defense:  Removal, Opponent Discard, Stax/Tax, Theft, Protection,
            Combat Tricks (1.6: tappers / combat rewriting),
            Recursion, Life Gain, Sacrifice, Goad, Pillowfort, Group Hug
  Themes:   Tribal, Spellslinger, Artifacts, Enchantments, Blink/ETB, Copy,
            Animator (0.9)
  Doubles:  Doubles Triggers, Doubles Spells, Doubles Tokens, Doubles
            Counters, Doubles Damage, Doubles Mana, Doubles Draw
  Traits:   Hard to Kill, Cheap Recast (3 or less), Instant Impact

16. DECK-LEVEL BONUSES FROM TAGS (2026-10-24)
=====================================================================
  These are added once per deck after all card points.

  WIN ROUTES (to Clock). A route counts only with real support:
    Combat (3+ threats / team pumps) | Drain/Burn (3+ non-combat damage
    cards) | Card Eater (3+) | Alt Win (1+ alt-win card) | Combo (2+
    finite combo pieces) | Poison (3+) | Commander Damage (commander has
    Game Plan: Voltron [Core])
    1 route = 0 | 2 = +0.25 | 3 = +0.6 | 4 = +1.0 | 5+ = +1.5

  COLOR STRAIN (to Mana). Fixing sources = lands that make 2+ colors,
    fetch lands, and cards tagged Fixing. Need 5 per color past the
    first (2 colors = 5, 5 colors = 20). -0.25 Mana per 3 sources short.

  REDUNDANCY (rules 1.2 - goes to CONSISTENCY only).
    Every deck gets a base of 1.5 CO. Count the cards in each ROLE below.
    For each role with 5-9 cards the base gets +6%; with 10+ cards, +8%.
    Redundancy = 1.5 x (1 + all boosts). Example: 2 roles at 10+ and
    4 at 5-9 = 1.5 x (1 + 0.16 + 0.24) = 2.1 CO.
    Ramp and Draw are NOT roles here - they have their own deck limits
    (best 14 ramp / best 12 draw full, the rest half).
    ROLES (18):
      Spot removal (creature/noncreature one-offs, bounce)
      Repeatable removal (removal engines, Provoke)
      Wipes & edicts
      Denial (counterspells, opponent discard, taxes/punishers, GY hate)
      Theft
      Tutors & selection (tutors, scry/surveil/loot)
      Tokens, copies & animators (Many Threats, Makes Token, Clone/Copy)
      Protection & sac outlets
      Recursion (reanimate, return to hand, self-recurring)
      Life gain
      Evasion (unblockable, menace, landwalk, other evasion, evasion
        givers - plain flying/trample don't count, nearly every deck
        has 8+)
      Drain & burn (non-combat damage, poison)
      Card eaters
      Team pumps (Buffer Offense)
      Finishers & combos (overrun, damage doubler, extra combat/turn,
        alt win, combo piece)
      Doublers (doublers and adders)
      Goad & deterrents (redirect, deterrent, Banding Defense)
      Combat tappers (Combat Tapper family tag - 1.6)
      Deal-making (bargain chip, vote/gift, group hug)
    Flags never count. Scary Threat tags don't count.
    Considered and REJECTED (1.2): an extra +10% when roles match the
    commander's game plan - being on-plan is already paid by archetype
    depth and the commander Game Plan multiplier.

  COMMANDER LEVELS stay a judgment call (Core / Supported / Side), using
  the family counts above only as a guide - see Section 15.

17. DOUBLERS AND "DOUBLED" CARDS (v2.2, 2026-10-24)
=====================================================================
  DOUBLERS get a flat base value only. They NEVER get Doubled tags.
  [B] Doubler (permanent) ................. 0.5 in the stat of what it
        doubles (tokens -> SU, counters -> CL, triggers -> stat most of
        its doubled triggers feed, mana -> MA, draw -> CA, spells -> CA)
  [B] Doubler (one-off, e.g. Second Harvest) 0.25
  [B] Adder (adds extra, doesn't double:
        Chatterfang, Peregrin, Donatello, Doc Samson) half of the above
  Each doubler/adder is written with its SCOPE - what it can double:
      Doubler: Tokens | Counters | All Triggers | Attack Triggers |
      Death Triggers | ETB Triggers | Type Triggers (dwarves, legends...)
      | Activated Abilities (Gogo, Echo) | Spells (copies what you cast)
      | Mana | Draw | Food/Clue/Treasure Tokens (Academy Manufactor)
      | Treasure Tokens (Treasure only: Jolene, Xorn - added 2026-10-24,
        scope label only, no point change)
  Damage Doubler stays its own flat tag (1.5 CL, Section 8).

  DOUBLED CARDS - cards WITHOUT any doubler/adder tag that a doubler in
  the same deck can boost (a token maker with Parallel Lives in the
  deck, an attack-trigger card with an Isshin-style doubler, etc.).
  [x] Doubled N ........................... LAST step for that card:
        its points x (1 + 0.05 per N)
      N counts each doubler/adder in the deck whose SCOPE hits the card:
        doubler in the 99 = 1 | commander doubler = 2 (always there)
        adder = 0.5
      Example: token maker in a deck with Doubling Season + Parallel
      Lives = Doubled 2 = x1.10.
      LANDS NEVER GET DOUBLED. Mana doublers (Mana Flare, Heartbeat of
      Spring, etc.) keep only their own flat base and give no Doubled
      tags to anything.

19. BANDING AND PROVOKE (rules 1.4, buffed in 1.5, 2026-10-24)
=====================================================================
  BANDING - a band attacks or blocks as a group, and YOU split the
  enemy creature's combat damage among your creatures. Two jobs, two
  tags (a banding card usually gets both). PICK ONE level for each.
  [B] Banding Offense Basic ............... 0.2 CL + 0.1 SU
  [B] Banding Offense Proficient .......... 0.35 CL + 0.2 SU
        attacking in a band: blockers can't pick off your best attacker,
        and a big non-bander can ride along in the band.
        Proficient = the bander also has first strike or flying (a
        flying band can only be blocked by fliers).
  [B] Banding Defense Basic ............... 0.3 SU + 0.15 TP
  [B] Banding Defense Proficient .......... 0.6 SU + 0.3 TP
        blocking with a bander: you spread the attacker's damage so your
        blockers live and the attacker dies - attacking you is a bad deal.
        Proficient = the bander also has first strike or 4+ toughness.
        Banding Defense replaces Deterrent on the same card (don't tag
        both for the banding). Counts in Redundancy role Goad & deterrents.
  Granted banding ({1}: gains banding, Icatian Infantry) = Basic.

  SHADOW (1.5) - tag it Unblockable (0.5 RE), not Evasion Other: in
  Commander almost nothing has shadow, so it can't be blocked.

  PROVOKE (pseudo-removal) - every attack, target creature untaps and
  must block the provoker. If the provoker wins that fight it's a
  repeatable kill; if not, it still pulls a blocker off your other
  attackers (and sets up "attacking or blocking" pingers like Heavy
  Ballista). PICK ONE.
  [B] Provoke Basic ....................... 0.3 IN + 0.2 RE
        provoker only kills small things or just survives (Swooping
        Talon 2/6, Deftblade Elite that prevents its own damage).
  [B] Provoke Proficient .................. 0.6 IN + 0.3 RE
        provoker usually wins the fight: first strike, deathtouch, or
        4+ power (Lowland Tracker).
  Not tagged as evasion (pass set 5 rule stands). Counts in Redundancy
  role Repeatable removal. Not "Aimable" by itself (the target is forced
  to block you, not chosen to hurt someone else).

20. COMBAT TAPPER FAMILY (rules 1.6, 2026-10-24)
=====================================================================
  [+] Combat Tapper ....................... 0.15 TP
      A permanent with a REPEATABLE activated ability (usually {T}) that
      kills, taps or shuts down creatures, or rewrites a combat:
      ping/destroy/exile an attacker or blocker (Heavy Ballista, Elite
      Archers, Catapult Master, Hand of Justice), tap a creature
      (Diversionary Tactics), remove a creature from combat (Maze of Ith,
      Reconnaissance, Labyrinth of Skophos), make an attacker blocked
      (Trap Runner), swap blockers (General Jarkeld), extra blocks
      (Mounted Archers), or move/prevent/reflect combat damage (Oracle's
      Attendants, Lancers en-Kor, Shield Dancer).
      It's ADDED on top of the card's real job tags (removal engine,
      Gives Protection, Deterrent...). The 0.15 Politics is the "nobody
      wants to swing into open tappers" pressure.
      Not for one-shot effects, triggers (Captain America's Shield), or
      mana abilities.
      Counts in its own Redundancy role: Combat tappers (19 roles now).
      Commanders can take Game Plan: Combat Tricks.

21. ANIMATOR FAMILY (rules 0.9, 2026-10-24)
=====================================================================
  Cards that turn your OTHER noncreature permanents (artifacts,
  enchantments, lands, vehicles) into creatures. PICK ONE.
  [B] Animator Basic ...................... 0.45 TH + 0.2 CL + 0.15 SU (0.9.1 boost, kept)
        one permanent at a time, or only until end of turn
        (Llanowar Loamspeaker's land, Peacewalker Colossus crewing a
        vehicle, Cyberdrive Awakener's one-turn 4/4 army).
  [B] Animator Proficient ................. 0.95 TH + 0.6 CL + 0.6 SU (0.9.1 boost, kept)
        a whole group STAYS animated (Bello on your turns, Opalescence
        style), or it makes a new lasting creature every turn (Iron Man,
        Futurist Paragon; Donatello, Mutant Mechanic).
        The Survival part: animated permanents are threats that dodge
        creature removal and wipes while they're not creatures.
  Rules:
   - Animator replaces Many Threats / Buffer Offense for the SAME
     effect (Bello was a token-maker; it makes no tokens).
   - Cards that only animate THEMSELVES (Haunted Screen, vehicles that
     crew themselves, man-lands) don't take it - use their own tags.
   - Token copies that become creatures (Ultron) stay Makes Token.
   - An animator that makes 0/0s or needs a count you don't have
     (Sage of the Maze with no Gates) gets nothing.
  Counts in the Redundancy role "Tokens, copies & animators" and as a
  Combat win-route piece. Commanders can take Game Plan: Animator.

22. ON-ATTACK SURVIVAL (rules 0.9.5, 2026-10-24)
=====================================================================
  For cards whose value comes from their OWN attack trigger ("whenever
  this creature / equipped creature attacks"), how likely is the
  attacker to live through combat and attack again next turn?
  [x] On Attack: Safe .......... x1.15 on the card's points
        flying / menace / unblockable with 3+ toughness, toughness 5+,
        indestructible, a big evasive vehicle, or it's going on a
        protected voltron commander (Kotis)
  [x] On Attack: Even .......... x1.0 (tracked only)
        average body: 3-4 toughness on the ground, small fliers,
        equipment that can move to whoever survives
  [x] On Attack: Risky ......... x0.85
        2 toughness or less with no evasion or protection - it trades
        or gets chumped, so the trigger may happen only once
  Triggers on "whenever YOU attack" (any creature) don't take it.
