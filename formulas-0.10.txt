POD RUNDOWN - SCORING FORMULAS  (0.10 - Oct 28, 2026 - ground-up rebuild, pre-release)
=====================================================================
Replaces the 0.9.12 tag system (Card Tags + Scoring Key). The old files
are archived, not deleted. Nothing has been converted yet: no deck has
been tagged or scored on these formulas.

HOW TO READ THIS
  - Every card gets a short tag per job it does, written with the
    numbers the formula needs, e.g.  Ramp (2) (MV:1).
  - A card scores fully in every stat it truly fits.
  - "Engine" = a permanent that sticks and keeps doing it.
    "One-Off" = happens once (spell, ETB, single trigger).
  - Estimates (amounts, usual board, usual X) are made for THAT deck.
  - Divide numbers (points needed for a 10 in each stat) are set later,
    once decks are tagged.

THE NINE STATS
  ENGINE   Mana . Cards . Consistency
  OFFENSE  Threats . Finish (was Clock) . Reach
  DEFENSE  Interaction . Survival . Politics

=====================================================================
1. MANA
=====================================================================
1a. RAMP   tag: Ramp (Amount) (MV:_)
  On every card that makes mana, except most lands. A land only gets
  Ramp if it makes more than one mana more often than not.
  Amount:
    - mana it makes, minus any mana paid to activate it (net)
    - lands put onto the battlefield = 1 each, lands to hand = 0.5 each
    - Treasure / mana tokens = 1 each
    - engines count what they make per turn
    - estimate when not exact
  Score:
    0.50 base
    +0.50 per Amount
    +0.25 usable immediately (haste, artifact)
    +0.25 no extra cost or condition to use it (separate from the above)
    +0.00 / +0.25 / +0.50  colorless / a set color / any color the deck needs
    +0.25 makes 2+ mana in any combination of colors
    +0.25 puts at least 1 land onto the battlefield
    -0.25 per MV above 1
    LAST: One-Off (Lasting - what it makes stays, like lands) x0.75
          One-Off (Temporary - mana used up: rituals, Treasure,
                   one-turn untaps) x0.5
          Engine - no cut

1b. MANA BOOST   tag: Ramp (Boost) (Amount) (MV:_)
  Cost Reducer, Untapper, Extra Land Drop. Runs through the Ramp formula.
  Amount = estimated mana saved or gained per turn in this deck:
    cost reducer = reduction x spells it covers per turn
    untapper     = mana from what it untaps
    extra land drop = about 0.5 per turn
  "No condition" bonus fails if it only covers some spells.
  Boost cards SKIP the color, mixed-colors and land-onto-battlefield
  bonuses (they speed up mana you already have).
  Nonland FIXING adds +0.40 per extra deck color it fixes x color
  factor (max 1.0) on top of its ramp score.

1c. CHEAT   tag: Cheat (Avg MV:_) (MV:_)
  Cheat Into Play, cascade, free casts.
    0.50 base
    +0.25 per mana saved (average MV it cheats in this deck - what you
          pay to use it)
    +0.25 you choose what it cheats (not random like cascade)
    +0.25 the cheated card stays (not sacrificed/exiled at end of turn)
    -0.25 per MV above 1
    LAST: One-Off (Lasting) x0.75 / One-Off (Temporary) x0.5 / Engine no cut

1d. LANDS
  Tags: kind (Basic Land / Multicolor Land / Fetch Land / Utility Land /
        Man-Land), entry (Untapped / Tapped / Untapped (_%) for
        conditional lands), and (Colors:_).
  BASIC LANDS ALWAYS SCORE 0 (they still count for land count and the
  land-drop simulation). Lands are worth slightly less than ramp.
  Step 1:
    +0.40 per extra deck color it makes beyond the first x color factor
          (mono 0 / 2 colors 0.5 / 3 colors 0.75 / 4-5 colors 1)
          any-color lands count all deck colors; fetches count the colors
          they can find
    +0.15 flexible (MDFC - spell side scores its own tags x0.5 - or
          cycling land)
    +0.05 nonbasic with basic land types, if the deck runs 3+ cards that
          fetch by type
    -0.05 costs life or damage to use
  Step 2 entry: Untapped x1 / Conditional x(0.5 + 0.5 x chance it enters
          untapped in this deck) / Tapped x0.5
  Step 3: cap 1.0 per land
  Utility / man-land abilities take their normal tag in their own stat
  (Conditional if they cost mana). Lands making 2+ mana use Ramp.

1e. MANA BASE CHECK (once per deck)
    Land count:     +0.5 if 36+ (or 33+ with 12+ ramp) / -0.5 if under 33
    Untapped share: +0.25 if 75%+ untapped / -0.25 if under 50%
    Color Strain:   -0.25 per 3 fixing sources short
                    (5 sources needed per color past the first)
    Land drops (simulation, 70%+ of games):
                    +0.5 every drop through turn 6
                    +0.75 if it also keeps going through turn 8

  Test: Through the Forest Gate 2.25 . Elvish Piper 2.25 . Sol Ring 2.0
        Cultivate 1.5 . Llanowar Elves 1.5 . Exploration 1.25
        Seedborn Muse 1.0 . Command Tower 0.60 (3c) / 1.0 (5c)
        Woodland Cemetery 0.26 . Temple of Mystery 0.15 . Forest 0

2. CARDS
=====================================================================
  tag: Draw (Amount) (MV:_)  + source: Hand / Second Hand / Opponent Gas
  Amount:
    - cards gained (drawn or made playable) minus cards discarded
    - one-offs count the total; engines count per turn cycle
    - impulse / top-of-library / opponents' cards count only what you'd
      actually play
    - Clues and card tokens = 1 each
    - blink engines count the cards their reused ETBs draw
    - X spells use the deck's usual turn-7 X
    - counts up to 7 (max hand size) unless the card removes it
  Score:
    0.25 base
    +0.50 per Amount
    +0.75 Engine (repeats every turn cycle)
    +0.25 usable immediately (instant, flash, no tapping)
    +0.25 no extra cost per card (Clues fail)
    Source: +0 Hand / +0.25 Second Hand / +0.50 Opponent Gas
    +0.25 free cast (play the cards without paying)
    +0.50 Wheel (whole new hand)
    -0.50 this-turn-only (impulse that expires)
    -0.25 restricted (lands only, creatures only, MV limit)
    -0.25 per MV above 1 (X spells: only the non-X part)
    LAST: Symmetric x0.75 (skipped if the deck profits when opponents
          draw, e.g. wheel-burn) . One-Off x0.75 . Engine no cut

  Test: Finale of Revelation 2.25 (X=5) . Rhystic Study 2.0
        Villainous Wealth 1.69 . Windfall 1.31 (Vile) / 0.98 (elsewhere)
        Tireless Tracker 1.25 . Elven Chorus 1.0 . Act on Impulse 0.75
        Frantic Search 0.19 (its value is in Consistency + Ramp)

3. CONSISTENCY
=====================================================================
3a. TUTOR   tag: Tutor (Reach) (MV:_)
    0.25 base
    Where it goes: +0.25 top of library / +0.50 hand / +0.75 battlefield
    Reach: +0 narrow (one name, lands only) / +0.25 a card type or
           tribe / +1.00 any card
    +0.25 instant speed
    +0.25 no extra cost
    +0.75 Engine
    -0.25 slow engine (needs to tap or survive a turn first)
    -0.50 per MV above 1
    LAST: One-Off x0.75 . Engine no cut

3b. SELECTION   tag: Select (N) (MV:_)
    0.25 base
    +0.15 per card scried / surveiled
    +0.05 per card dug ("look at the top N, take one")
    +0.25 per card looted / rummaged
    +0.25 instant speed
    +0.25 no extra cost
    +0.75 Engine
    -0.25 slow engine
    -0.50 per MV above 1
    LAST: One-Off x0.75 . Engine no cut . on a land x0.5

3c. HIT-RATE (once per deck) - PENDING
    Idea: odds of having the deck's key cards by turn 4 and turn 6.
    Still undecided: whether to keep it, and how to pick the key cards
    by math only. Until decided, Consistency = Tutor + Selection points.

  Test: Vampiric Tutor 1.31 . Demonic Tutor 1.13 . Fauna Shaman 1.0
        Merfolk Looter 1.0 . Worldly Tutor 0.94 . Expedition Map 0.75
        Impulse 0.34 . Frantic Search 0.19 . Temple of Mystery 0.15

4. THREATS
=====================================================================
  MV:_ for Threats = printed MV, or EFFECTIVE MV for cards that reduce
  their own cost: printed MV - the reduction the deck usually has on
  turn 6, never below the card's colored pips (Ghalta -> MV:5).
  Covers affinity / convoke-style cards too.

4a. TALL   tag: Threat: Tall (Power) (MV:_)   (power 4+ or a Bomb only)
    0.05 base
    +0.15 per power (usual power in this deck)
    Pressure +0.25 (does something every turn alone) /
    Bomb +0.50 (takes over or ends the game alone)
    +0.25 hard to answer (hexproof, ward, indestructible on itself)
    +0.25 haste or flash
    -0.10 per MV above 1
    Conditional x0.75 (vehicles needing crew, "as long as")
    Noncreature must-answers use base + Pressure/Bomb only.

4b. WIDE   tag: Threat: Wide (Power made) (MV:_)
    0.05 base
    +0.25 per token power (a token with a keyword counts +1 power)
          one-off = total, engine = per turn cycle
    +0.50 Engine
    -0.25 if it costs something each time (life, sacrifice)
    -0.10 per MV above 1
    LAST: One-Off x0.75

4c. BOARD POWER   tag: Threat: Board Power (+N x creatures) (MV:_)
    0.05 base
    +0.10 x boost x creatures it boosts (the deck's usual board)
    -0.10 per MV above 1
    Static anthems / permanent counters = Engine (no cut);
    one-time team counters x0.75

  Pressure only counts for effects not already scored in another
  Threats part. Token copies take the tag of what they copy.
  THREAT VARIETY (once per deck): kinds = Tall, Wide, Board Power,
  noncreature must-answer, commander threat. 4+ kinds +1 . 1 kind -1

  Test: Ghalta 1.95 . The Thing 1.51 . Grave Titan 1.5
        Secure the Wastes 1.16 . Tendershoot Dryad 1.15 . Hornet Queen 1.09
        Sheoldred, the Apocalypse 0.85 . Bitterblossom 0.7
        Glorious Anthem 0.45 . Colossal Dreadmaw 0.45

5. FINISH  (was Clock)
=====================================================================
  tag: Finish (Type) (Amount) (MV:_)
  Types: Overrun . Extra Combat . Extra Turn . Damage Doubler . Burst .
         Alt Win . Combo Piece
  Amount = extra damage added that turn vs the deck's usual turn-8 board:
    Overrun        boost x creatures (half without trample)
    Extra Combat   usual board power
    Extra Turn     usual board power + flat +0.75
    Damage Doubler extra damage per turn
    Burst          total damage / life loss to PLAYERS (usual X)
  Score:
    0.05 base
    +0.06 per damage up to 20
    +0.03 per damage from 21 to 60 (nothing counts past 60)
    Alt Win: flat +1.50 instead of Amount
    Combo Piece: flat +0.50 per piece, if the combo is lethal or a win
    +0.30 Burst (can't be blocked)
    +0.50 Engine
    +0.25 instant speed / flash
    -0.25 if each use costs mana again
    -0.10 per MV above 1 (effective MV for self-reducers)
    Symmetric x0.75 . Conditional x0.75 . Hard Conditional x0.5
    No One-Off cut (a finisher only needs to happen once).

  Test: Craterhoof Behemoth 1.42 . Fiery Emancipation 1.37
        Exsanguinate 1.33 (X=6) . Time Warp 1.12 . Aggravated Assault 0.82
        Approach of the Second Sun 0.48 . Relentless Assault 0.47

=====================================================================
6. REACH
=====================================================================
  Evasion levels (best one, +0.1 if two kinds):
    can't be blocked x1.0 . shadow/fear/intimidate/skulk x0.8
    flying x0.7 . menace x0.6 . trample x0.5 . landwalk x0.4

6a. EVASIVE   tag: Reach: Evasive (Power x Level)
    0.05 base
    +0.10 per power that gets through (usual power x level)
    No MV penalty (the body paid it in Threats)

6b. GIVES EVASION   tag: Reach: Gives Evasion (Power x Level) (MV:_)
    0.05 base
    +0.10 per power made evasive (one creature = usual best attacker,
          team = usual board)
    +0.50 Engine
    -0.25 if each use costs mana again
    -0.10 per MV above 1
    LAST: One-Off x0.75 . Conditional x0.75

6c. DRAIN / PING   tag: Reach: Drain (Damage per cycle) (MV:_)
    0.05 base
    +0.15 per expected damage / life loss to the table per turn cycle
          (using this deck's fuel: deaths, creatures entering, spells)
    -0.10 per MV above 1
    Needs Fodder / Conditional x0.75 when the deck is short on the fuel

  POISON: each poison counter = 3 damage.
  CARD EATER (opponent mill): 2 cards milled = 1 damage.
    Steady mill engines -> Reach as Drain.
    Big one-shot mill   -> Finish as Burst (with the +0.30 bonus).

  Test: Purphoros 1.1 . Levitation 1.09 . Sheoldred (drain) 0.8
        Rogue's Passage 0.68 . Ghalta 0.65 . The Thing 0.5
        Blood Artist 0.4 . Blighted Agent 0.35 . Serra Angel 0.33

7. INTERACTION
=====================================================================
  tag: Answer (Type) (MV:_)   Types: Removal . Counter . Wipe . Denial
    0.05 base
    Removal: bounce +0.25 / damage or edict +0.40 / destroy +0.50 /
             exile or permanent steal +0.75
    Hits: two or more types +0.25 / any permanent +0.50
    Extra targets: 2nd +0.50, then +0.25 each
    Counter: hard +0.75 / soft +0.40 . counters any spell +0.25
    Wipe: first in deck +1.00, later +0.50 . exile +0.25 .
          one-sided +0.50 . bounce wipe -0.25
    Denial (stax, taxes, graveyard hate, discard): +0.25 .
          one-sided +0.25 . +0.25 per card discarded
    +0.25 instant speed
    +0.25 no extra cost
    +0.50 Engine
    -0.15 per MV above 1
    Conditional x0.75
    MODAL / OVERLOAD: every mode scores in full
    LAST: Interaction scale x0.45
  COVERAGE (once per deck): +1 if it answers 4+ kinds (creature,
    artifact, enchantment, planeswalker, graveyard, spell) .
    -1 if it only hits creatures

  Test: Cyclonic Rift 0.92 . Pick Your Poison 0.77 . Return to Dust 0.72
        Counterspell 0.63 . Assassin's Trophy 0.63
        Swords to Plowshares 0.59 . Beast Within 0.56
        Tragic Arrogance 0.54

8. SURVIVAL
=====================================================================
  tag: Survive (Part) (MV:_)   Parts: Protect . Recover . Endure
    0.05 base
  PROTECT
    gives protection to one creature +0.40 / several or the board +0.75
    hexproof, shroud or protection +0.25 . indestructible +0.25
    its own survival keywords +0.10 each
    sac outlet +0.25 (+0.25 more if free)
  RECOVER
    return or reanimate one card +0.50, +0.25 each extra
    mass recursion +1.50 (+0.75 if symmetric)
    comes back on its own (undying etc.) +0.40
    Rebuilder (token engines) +0.15 per body per turn
  ENDURE
    life gain +0.15 per 3 life per turn cycle (one-off counts half)
    fog +0.40 . ongoing damage prevention +0.50
    blocker +0.05 per toughness (defender or 4+ toughness),
            +0.25 deathtouch / first strike, +0.15 blocks flyers
  EVERYONE
    +0.25 instant speed . +0.50 Engine . -0.10 per MV above 1

  Test: Akroma's Will 1.25 . Boros Charm 1.2 . Snakeskin Veil 0.95
        Wall of Reverence 0.9 . Meren of Clan Nel Toth 0.75
        Tendershoot Dryad 0.75 . Prismatic Strands 0.75 . Reanimate 0.55

9. POLITICS
=====================================================================
  tag: Politics (Lever) (MV:_)   Levers: Deals . Deterrents . Direction
    0.20 base
  DEALS (what you can offer)
    lopsided group hug +0.40 . vote or gift +0.40 .
    monarch or initiative +0.40
    Bargain Chip (removal/abilities that can be aimed at opponents):
      skips the base, flat +0.10
  DETERRENTS (what you threaten)
    pillow fort ("can't attack you") +0.60 . attack tax +0.50 .
    punisher / deterrent / combat tapper / opponent tax +0.30
  DIRECTION (where attacks and answers go)
    goad one +0.30 (+0.25 each extra target) .
    goad all or forced attacks +0.75 . redirect +0.40
  EVERYONE
    +0.25 instant speed . +0.50 Engine . -0.10 per MV above 1 .
    purely symmetric x0.5
  ONCE PER DECK
    Lever spread: +0.50 for each lever with 3+ cards
    Salt nudge: deck salt under 20 +1 / 20-40 0 / 40-60 -1 / over 60 -2

  Test: Mystic Remora 1.0 . Curse of Disturbance 0.9
        Curse of Verbosity 0.9 . Kynaios and Tiro 0.8 . Monologue Tax 0.8
        Rhystic Study (tax) 0.8 . Taunt from the Rampart 0.55
        Martyr's Bond 0.5 . Swords to Plowshares (bargain chip) 0.1

==================== RULES 0.10.1 (Oct 28, 2026) ====================
TUTOR: new destination "Graveyard" = same as Hand (+0.50), only in decks that use cards from the graveyard.
       +0.25 for each extra card found (Buried Alive finds 3: +0.50).
FEEDS THE PLAN (new): +0.15 once / +0.25 every turn, added to the card's best stat, when it triggers the
       commander or a core engine. Thing & Kykar: noncreature spells MV 3 or less. Turtle: tokens (Leonardo).
       Joel: tokens/bodies (drain). Mech: artifacts. Marina: enchantments. Kotis: auras/equipment/mutate.
       Mathas: paying your own life. Grim: graveyard fuel for the Throne. The engine cards themselves don't get it.
BODY FLOOR (new): a creature scoring under 0.50 also counts its body:
       Threats 0.05 + 0.03 x power (max 4); Survival 0.02 x toughness (max 6).
LATER WIPES: every wipe after the first is worth x0.60 (was a flat -0.5 before the x0.45 scale).
GROWERS: creatures that grow every turn are tagged at their grown power (Michelangelo 4, Swiftspear 4,
       Frolicking Familiar 4, Crackdown Construct 5, Tempest Hart 5), conditional x0.75 where it needs setup.
POLITICS: + Aim: single-target removal +0.10 (Direction lever), up to 8 cards.
          + Counterspells +0.15 (Deals lever), up to 5 cards.
          Rating uses the square root of the final Politics points (divide = second-highest deck, as always).
MANA DIVIDE: fixed at 4.00 (every other stat still uses second-highest deck = 10).

==================== RULES 0.10.2 (Oct 28, 2026) ====================
BLOODTHIRST: Threat: Tall at power + N counters (X = the deck's usual damage on its own turn, Grim = 6), x0.75 condition.
TUTOR MV PENALTY (trial): -0.25 per MV above 1 (was -0.5).

==================== RULES 0.10.3 (Oct 28, 2026) ====================
SECOND LIFE (graveyard abilities): flashback, encore, cast-from-graveyard, "exile this card from your graveyard: ..."
  (incl. giving its abilities/keywords to another creature). Score the graveyard effect with the normal formula
  x0.60 (x0.40 if it also costs a card), plus 0.15 Cards for the extra use.

==================== RULES 0.10.4 (Oct 28, 2026) ====================
PLAN CARDS: 16-22 hand-picked cards per deck that make it do its thing; each marked 1 / 0.5 / 0 for how well it works
  without the commander.
HIT-RATE (Consistency): K = plan cards (+0.25 more if it has Second Life) + tutors (1) + selection cards (0.5).
  +3 x P(at least 1 of K in your first 10 cards) + 1 x P(at least 2).
PLAN B (Consistency): +1.5 x P(at least 1 commander-independent plan card in 10 cards), using the 1/0.5/0 weights.
ANSWER ON TIME (Interaction): +1.0 x P(at least 1 answer in 10 cards).
CLOSER ON TIME (Finish): +0.75 x P(at least 1 finisher, 0.3+ Finish points, in 13 cards).
WIDE SATURATION (Threats): Wide points past 10 per deck count half (scaled evenly across every token maker).
THREAT VARIETY (fix): only Tall, Wide, Board Power, noncreature threats and Commander count as kinds
  (Body floor and Feeds the Plan entries don't).
NEEDS REPAIR replaces the old "Weak spots & fixes" box.

==================== RULES 0.10.5 (Oct 28, 2026) ====================
REBOUND: the free second cast next upkeep scores the effect again x0.80.
Full card-text review of all 9 decks (about 35 cards got missing tags; listed on the site Notes tab).

==================== RULES 0.10.6 (Oct 28, 2026) ====================

  Small (one player's spot flips) 0.25 | Medium (a player's life or board turns around) 0.50 |
  Large (the whole table resets) 0.75; +0.50 repeatable, +0.25 instant, -0.05 per MV above 3.
  Added on top of the card's other tags. Tagged: Axis of Mortality, Soul Conduit, Reverse the Sands, Profane
  Transfusion, Peer into the Abyss, Sower of Discord, Tragic Arrogance, Single Combat, Promise of Loyalty,
  Akroma's Will, Dire Fleet Ravager, Quietus Spike, Villainous Wealth, White Sun's Twilight, Second Harvest,
