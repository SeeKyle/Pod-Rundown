0.10 - Oct 28, 2026 - ground-up rebuild (pre-release)
  WHAT: Replaced the whole 0.9.12 tag system with per-stat formulas
        (see Pod_Rundown_Formulas_0.10.txt). Clock renamed Finish.
        Token swarms moved from Survival to Threats; Survival now
        Protect / Recover / Endure with blockers and fogs. Politics now
        scored by levers (Deals / Deterrents / Direction) plus a salt
        nudge. Burn and mill split: one-shot -> Finish, steady -> Reach.
        All deck tags and scores dropped; decklists reset to plain
        card lists.
  WHY:  The old system grew piece by piece (doublers, squash, routing,
        redundancy) and got hard to follow. The rebuild gives every
        stat one readable formula, tested on real pod cards.
  ARCHIVED: Pod_Rundown_Card_Tags_0.9.12.txt,
            Pod_Rundown_Scoring_Key_0.9.12.txt (in Archive/).
